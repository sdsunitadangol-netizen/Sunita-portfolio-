FAQ

Common Questions

How early should we book?

At least 2 to 4 weeks ahead for groups of 10 or more. If you are traveling between October and November, book earlier as that is Nepal's peak season and accommodation fills up quickly.

Can we customize our itinerary?

Completely. We build every group tour around what your group actually wants. Destinations, pace, and activities are all flexible. There are no fixed packages.

Do Indian travelers need a visa?

No. Indian nationals can enter Nepal with any valid government issued photo ID. Most other nationalities get a visa on arrival at Kathmandu airport.

Is Nepal safe for large groups?

Yes. Nepal is one of the most group friendly destinations in Asia. Our guides are trained in first aid and we recommend travel insurance that covers medical evacuation.
