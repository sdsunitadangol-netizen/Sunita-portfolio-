Getting Started
How do I start planning my trip to Nepal? +

Send me a message using the contact form or WhatsApp with your travel dates, group size, and what kind of trip you are looking for. I will respond within 24 hours with options and a quote.

How far in advance should I book? +

For group tours of 10 or more, book at least 2 to 4 weeks ahead. For travel during October and November which is Nepal's peak trekking season, book 4 to 6 weeks in advance to secure permits and accommodation.

Can I customize my itinerary? +

Completely. Every trip is built around what you actually want. Destinations, duration, pace, and activities are all flexible. There are no fixed packages.

Visas and Entry
Do Indian travelers need a visa to enter Nepal? +

No. Indian nationals can enter Nepal with any valid government issued photo ID such as an Aadhaar card, voter ID, or passport. No visa is required.

Do travelers from Bangladesh need a visa? +

Yes. Bangladeshi nationals require a visa to enter Nepal. A visa on arrival is available at Tribhuvan International Airport in Kathmandu for a fee.

Tour Details
What is included in the tour price? +

This depends on the tour type. Generally included: airport pickup and drop off, accommodation, private vehicle, local guide, entry permits, and activity fees. Excluded: international flights, Nepal visa fees, personal expenses, tips, and travel insurance.

Is Nepal safe for solo female travelers? +

Yes. Nepal is considered one of the safer destinations in Asia for solo female travelers. I have helped many solo women travelers from India and Bangladesh experience Nepal safely and comfortably.

How do I pay? +

Payment details and methods will be confirmed once your trip is finalized. I work with common payment methods accessible from India and Bangladesh.

What if my plans change after booking? +

I understand travel plans can change. Reach out as early as possible and we will work together to adjust the itinerary or reschedule where possible.
